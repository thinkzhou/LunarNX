# Steam symbol

`steam.svg` is Valve's unmodified white Steam symbol, retrieved 2026-09-07 from
https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/store/about/icon-steamos.svg
(linked by https://store.steampowered.com/about/).

`romfs/img/platform/steam.png` is a 256 × 256 transparent rasterization of that
SVG, preserving its aspect ratio and artwork. It is used only to identify the
Steam Remote Play platform, not as LunarNX branding or a claim of endorsement.
Steam and the Steam logo are trademarks of Valve Corporation. This artwork is
not covered by LunarNX's source-code license. Branding guidance:
https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding
